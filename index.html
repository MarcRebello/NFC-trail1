What is an NFC card?

NFC (near-field communication) is the same tech behind tap-to-pay. A tiny chip inside the card holds a link. When a phone comes within a few centimetres, it reads the link and opens it.

  1. Hold the card

    You hand over your card, or just show it. Nothing to switch on and no battery to charge.

  2. Tap the phone

    Touch the top of their phone to the card. Most modern iPhones and Android phones read it right away.

  3. Your page opens

    Your contact details, links and portfolio appear. One tap saves you to their contacts.

Paper card vs NFC card

Both fit in a wallet. Only one can be updated after you print it.

Paper card

  • Typed in by hand, or lost in a drawer
  • Wrong phone number means a reprint
  • Fixed text only
  • No idea if anyone looked at it

NFC card

  • Saved to their phone in one tap
  • Change your details any time, same card
  • Link to your website, socials, menu or portfolio
  • Reusable for years

Questions beginners ask

Does the other person need an app?

No. Phones read NFC tags in the background and open the link in the browser.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, on recent models of both. Keep NFC switched on for Android phones that have it off.

What if the phone does not support NFC?

Print a QR code on the back of the card as a backup. It opens the same page.

Does the card need charging?

Never. The chip is powered by the phone when it gets close.
